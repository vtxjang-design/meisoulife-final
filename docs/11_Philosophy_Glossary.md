# Meisou Life Philosophy Glossary

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Source of truth for Foundation Pack terminology

## 1. Governance Rule

This glossary is the canonical source for core philosophical terms in Meisou Life documents, product language, AI prompts, and translations. If a term conflicts with another document, this glossary governs until the Foundation Pack is formally amended.

### 1.1 Official term constant

| Constant | Official value | Change rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `RETURN_TO_ORIGIN_HANJA` | `復本` | Do not use `複本` unless the founder formally adopts it. If adopted, update this constant, the definition below, and all dependent documents in one controlled revision. |

`復本` is selected because the current intended meaning is “returning to or restoring the original foundation.” It is not interchangeable with `複本`.

## 2. Core Terms

### Philosophical Source

- **Canonical Korean expression:** 일지 이승헌 스승님의 철학적 원류(지구경영과 뇌교육)
- **English expression:** The philosophical source of Ilchi Lee's Earth Management and Brain Education
- **Meaning:** The philosophical direction from which Meisou Life draws its understanding of Hongik, Brain Education, natural intelligence, Earth Citizenship, Earth Management, and coexistence.
- **Governance use:** Distinguish philosophical attribution from claims of endorsement, official participation, platform-founding status, or permission to use names and works.
- **Do not mean:** Personality worship, doctrinal authority over users, or an unverified public claim of official affiliation.

### Civilization Architect

- **Korean:** 문명설계자
- **English:** Civilization Architect
- **Current role:** Master Jang
- **Meaning:** The human role that translates the philosophical source into HROS, platform structure, product, technology, sustainable economics, community, and long-horizon Coexistence Civilization architecture.
- **Authority:** Final human approval for material philosophical, constitutional, platform, product, economic, and community-architecture decisions, bounded by law, professional duties, participant rights, safety, dignity, agency, and this Constitution.
- **Do not mean:** Spiritual rank, unrestricted personal authority, or exemption from governance safeguards.

### Gongsaeng AI

- **Korean:** 공생AI
- **English:** Gongsaeng AI
- **Concise definition:** The coordinated operating system of approved platform, content, coaching, development, economic, automation, and community tools that supports Meisou Life.
- **Current tool map:** Vercel; YouTube / Instagram / TikTok as one discovery layer; ChatGPT; Gemini; Codex; Stripe; LINE.
- **Do not mean:** A single chatbot, autonomous civilization designer, spiritual authority, or final decision-maker.

### Gongsaeng Coach

- **Korean:** 공생 코치
- **English:** Gongsaeng Coach
- **Concise definition:** The user-facing ChatGPT/Gemini module within Gongsaeng AI that supports awareness, recovery, self-directed choice, and daily practice.
- **Internal-system expression:** Follow HROS, the AI Oath, safety escalation, data minimization, truthful disclosure, and return-to-life principles.
- **Do not mean:** The whole Gongsaeng AI operating system, clinical care, spiritual diagnosis, or human authority.

### Recovery

- **Korean:** 회복
- **Hanja:** 回復 (when a Hanja form is needed)
- **English:** Recovery
- **Japanese:** 回復（かいふく）
- **Concise definition:** The ongoing return to inner balance, natural rhythm, conscious self-direction, relationship, and responsibility for life.
- **Extended philosophical definition:** Recovery is the process through which a person returns to Zero Consciousness, restores original nature, realigns with Yullyeo, and grows into a responsible participant in Earth Management and Coexistence Civilization. It includes but exceeds relief, rest, stress management, and personal healing.
- **User-facing expression:** “Take a small pause and return to your natural rhythm.”
- **Internal-system expression:** “Design for recovery before optimization; evaluate agency, rhythm, relationship, and life-serving participation.”
- **Do not mean:** A clinical diagnosis, guaranteed cure, productivity upgrade, spiritual score, or a requirement to suppress emotion.

### Zero Consciousness

- **Korean:** 제로의식
- **Hanja:** 없음 (project term; no official Hanja assigned)
- **English:** Zero Consciousness
- **Japanese:** ゼロ意識
- **Concise definition:** An open, centered state before fixed identification, impulsive reaction, or excessive mental division.
- **Extended philosophical definition:** Zero Consciousness is a contemplative orientation that helps a person pause before habitual identification and re-contact present reality. It supports awareness and conscious choice; it is not an achievement to be certified.
- **User-facing expression:** “You can pause before reacting and notice what is here.”
- **Internal-system expression:** “Use only as an optional philosophical learning concept; never infer attainment from behavior or text.”
- **Do not mean:** Unconsciousness, emotional numbness, passivity, dissociation, escape from reality, medical condition, or enlightenment certificate.

### Return to Origin

- **Korean:** 복본
- **Hanja:** 復本
- **English:** Return to Origin
- **Japanese:** 本来への回帰（ほんらいへのかいき）
- **Concise definition:** Returning to and restoring one's original foundation.
- **Extended philosophical definition:** Return to Origin (復本) names reconnection with inherent dignity, vitality, clarity, compassion, responsibility, and capacity for conscious choice. It is a practice of restoration, not regression, purity, or withdrawal from ordinary life.
- **User-facing expression:** “Return to what helps you feel grounded and alive.”
- **Internal-system expression:** “Use `復本` consistently; connect recovery to dignity, agency, and participation in life.”
- **Do not mean:** Returning to a past identity, rejecting modern life, superiority over others, or a guaranteed spiritual event.

### Yullyeo

- **Korean:** 율려
- **Hanja:** 律呂
- **English:** Yullyeo
- **Japanese:** 律呂（りつりょ）
- **Concise definition:** A philosophical concept of the primordial rhythm and order of the universe and life.
- **Extended philosophical definition:** In Meisou Life, Yullyeo describes the living order with which a person seeks greater harmony through breath, sleep, movement, work, rest, food, emotion, relationship, nature, and technology use. It is philosophical language, not a measurable scientific, medical, or psychological diagnosis.
- **User-facing expression:** “Find a rhythm that helps your life feel more balanced.”
- **Internal-system expression:** “Translate Yullyeo into practical rhythm-supporting design without making scientific claims.”
- **Do not mean:** A proven medical mechanism, a frequency score, a rigid schedule, or evidence that a user is spiritually advanced.

### Natural Intelligence

- **Korean:** 자연지성
- **Hanja:** 自然知性
- **English:** Natural Intelligence
- **Japanese:** 自然知性（しぜんちせい）
- **Concise definition:** The human capacity to sense, learn, choose, and relate in participation with life.
- **Extended philosophical definition:** Natural Intelligence is the practical capacity that becomes more available when attention is not wholly captured by fear, impulse, comparison, or algorithms. It joins embodied awareness, discernment, compassion, and responsible action.
- **User-facing expression:** “Notice the wisdom already available in your body and daily life.”
- **Internal-system expression:** “Design for discernment and real-world agency, not AI substitution.”
- **Do not mean:** An IQ claim, biological determinism, anti-science, or superiority over other people.

### Consciousness Sovereignty / Brain Ownership

- **Korean:** 의식 주권 / 뇌 주권
- **Hanja:** 意識主權 / 腦主權
- **English:** Consciousness Sovereignty / Brain Ownership
- **Japanese:** 意識の主権（いしきのしゅけん）/ 脳の主権（のうのしゅけん）
- **Concise definition:** The ability to observe, choose, and direct attention, thought, action, and daily life.
- **Extended philosophical definition:** Consciousness Sovereignty is regained when a person is less governed by impulse, algorithms, fear, comparison, or external pressure and is more able to make informed, responsible choices. “Brain Ownership” is an adjacent operational expression, not a claim of literal ownership or neuroscience diagnosis.
- **User-facing expression:** “Choose what deserves your attention today.”
- **Internal-system expression:** “Provide controls, transparent choices, and non-manipulative UX that protect user agency.”
- **Do not mean:** Total control over thoughts or feelings, blame for distress, immunity from influence, or a medical claim.

### Cheon-Ji-In Spirit

- **Korean:** 천지인 정신
- **Hanja:** 天地人精神
- **English:** Cheon-Ji-In Spirit
- **Japanese:** 天地人の精神（てんちじんのせいしん）
- **Concise definition:** The spirit of interrelationship among Heaven, Earth, and Human.
- **Extended philosophical definition:** Cheon-Ji-In Spirit understands human life as existing within a larger living order. In Meisou Life, it connects personal recovery with care for others, nature, and future generations.
- **User-facing expression:** “Your wellbeing is connected with people, nature, and the world you share.”
- **Internal-system expression:** “Connect individual practice with ecological and social responsibility.”
- **Do not mean:** Religious coercion, human supremacy, or a claim that one tradition invalidates another.

### Earth Citizenship

- **Korean:** 지구시민 정신 / 지구시민
- **Hanja:** 地球市民精神 / 地球市民
- **English:** Earth Citizenship
- **Japanese:** 地球市民（ちきゅうしみん）
- **Concise definition:** An inclusive identity and responsibility as a member of the planetary community.
- **Extended philosophical definition:** Earth Citizenship complements local, national, cultural, and personal identities. It recognizes responsibilities to life beyond nationality, ideology, religion, ethnicity, or social status.
- **User-facing expression:** “Practice care for the wider world we share.”
- **Internal-system expression:** “Evaluate product and community choices for inclusive planetary responsibility.”
- **Do not mean:** Erasing local identity, political allegiance, or cultural difference.

### Earth Management

- **Korean:** 지구경영
- **Hanja:** 地球經營
- **English:** Earth Management
- **Japanese:** 地球経営（ちきゅうけいえい）
- **Concise definition:** Life-serving stewardship by recovered people across personal, social, technological, and civilizational systems.
- **Extended philosophical definition:** Earth Management is a practical philosophy through which recovered human beings direct their lives, communities, technologies, institutions, and civilization toward life, coexistence, sustainability, and the wellbeing of future generations.
- **User-facing expression:** “Let your choices support people, nature, and the future.”
- **Internal-system expression:** “Apply life dignity, consciousness sovereignty, natural harmony, community responsibility, and future-generation responsibility to material decisions.”
- **Do not mean:** Controlling, owning, dominating, or administering Earth as an object.

### Coexistence

- **Korean:** 공생
- **Hanja:** 共生
- **English:** Coexistence
- **Japanese:** 共生（きょうせい）
- **Concise definition:** Living and flourishing together in reciprocal responsibility.
- **Extended philosophical definition:** Coexistence applies recovery beyond the individual to relationships, communities, society, nature, and technology. It seeks mutual benefit without erasing difference or avoiding accountability.
- **User-facing expression:** “Practice living well together.”
- **Internal-system expression:** “Design relationships and systems for consent, reciprocity, repair, and reduced extraction.”
- **Do not mean:** Passive agreement, tolerance of harm, forced harmony, or loss of boundaries.

### Coexistence Civilization

- **Korean:** 공생문명
- **Hanja:** 共生文明
- **English:** Coexistence Civilization
- **Japanese:** 共生文明（きょうせいぶんめい）
- **Concise definition:** A civilization organized around the dignity of life and regenerative coexistence.
- **Extended philosophical definition:** Coexistence Civilization is the long-horizon social expression of recovery, consciousness sovereignty, Earth Citizenship, and Earth Management across institutions, culture, technology, and ecological practice.
- **User-facing expression:** “Help build a future where life can flourish together.”
- **Internal-system expression:** “Use as a strategic direction, never as a claim that Meisou Life alone can deliver civilization-scale change.”
- **Do not mean:** A finished utopia, ideological conformity, or brand promise of social transformation.

### Popularization of Enlightenment

- **Korean:** 깨달음의 대중화
- **Hanja:** 覺醒의 大衆化 (descriptive; not a required formal term)
- **English:** Popularization of Enlightenment
- **Japanese:** 悟りの大衆化（さとりのたいしゅうか）
- **Concise definition:** Making opportunities for reflection, awareness, and conscious living accessible in ordinary life.
- **Extended philosophical definition:** The term describes broad access to practices that support awareness and responsible living. It does not market enlightenment as a product, guarantee awakening, or establish a hierarchy between people.
- **User-facing expression:** “Make space for awareness in everyday life.”
- **Internal-system expression:** “Increase access to optional contemplative practice while preventing attainment claims and coercive engagement.”
- **Do not mean:** Mass certification of enlightenment, a spiritual credential, or a guaranteed outcome.
