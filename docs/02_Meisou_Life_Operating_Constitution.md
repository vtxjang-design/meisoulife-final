# Meisou Life Operating Constitution

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Foundational governance standard

## 1. Authority and Purpose

This Constitution establishes the decision-making principles for Meisou Life. It exists to ensure that the organization, its products, its community, and its AI systems remain aligned with the Meisou Life DNA.

It applies to founders, leaders, employees, contractors, advisors, partners, moderators, and AI-enabled operations. Commercial success is necessary for continuity, but it is never a justification for violating this Constitution.

## 2. Constitutional Order

When priorities conflict, decisions follow this order:

1. Human dignity, safety, and agency.
2. Ecological responsibility and the long-term conditions for life.
3. Trust, fairness, and community wellbeing.
4. Product quality, operational resilience, and sustainable economics.
5. Growth, reach, and short-term optimization.

No lower priority may override a higher one without a documented, time-bound exception approved through the governance process.

## 3. Rights of Participants

Every participant in Meisou Life has the right to:

- Be treated with respect, without discrimination or humiliation.
- Understand what is being offered, what it costs, and how their data is used.
- Make voluntary choices, including the choice to pause or leave.
- Receive clear disclosure when interacting with AI or automated systems.
- Seek correction, report harm, and receive a meaningful response.
- Have personal information handled with care and proportionality.

## 4. Duties of Stewards

Anyone acting on behalf of Meisou Life must:

- Place recovery and agency before extraction and engagement.
- Communicate clearly, especially about limitations, tradeoffs, and uncertainty.
- Avoid dark patterns, misleading claims, and pressure-based selling.
- Raise foreseeable risks early and participate in repair when harm occurs.
- Respect confidentiality and use only the access necessary for their role.
- Treat feedback, dissent, and lived experience as valuable governance input.

## 5. Product and Content Governance

Products and content must be calm, accessible, and proportionate to their purpose. They must not present spiritual practice as a guaranteed cure, clinical treatment, or moral obligation.

They must not score, rank, certify, or infer recovery, consciousness, awakening, enlightenment, or spiritual maturity. Philosophy and contemplative practice must remain clearly distinct from medical and psychological care.

Before a material release, the responsible owner documents: intended benefit, affected users, known risks, data implications, safeguards, accessibility needs, rollback plan, and success measures beyond engagement.

## 6. AI Governance

AI systems are subject to the AI Oath and AI Board review. High-impact uses—including emotionally sensitive guidance, personalization based on sensitive data, automated restrictions, or material user decisions—require documented human oversight and a clear escalation path.

## 7. Data and Privacy

Data is entrusted, not owned. Meisou Life uses data only for legitimate, communicated purposes; minimizes collection and retention; limits access; and protects sensitive information with heightened care. Personal reflection, emotional disclosures, and practice history are never treated as advertising inventory.

## 8. Community Safety and Repair

Community spaces protect belonging, consent, and dignity. Reports of harm are received without retaliation, assessed fairly, and addressed in proportion to their seriousness. Repair may include clarification, apology, content correction, access restriction, referral, or process change.

## 9. Decision Process

Significant decisions use the following record:

| Required element | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Purpose | State the human and organizational need. |
| Stakeholders | Identify people, communities, and systems affected. |
| Risks | Identify foreseeable harms and uncertainty. |
| Alternatives | Consider a smaller, safer, or more regenerative option. |
| Safeguards | Define limits, owners, and escalation paths. |
| Review | Set a date and measurable conditions for reassessment. |

## 10. Exceptions

Exceptions are rare, written, narrowly scoped, and time-bound. They must name the constitutional principle affected, the reason, the mitigation, the accountable owner, and the expiry date. Emergency action may proceed first only when delay would create greater harm; it must be reviewed promptly afterward.

## 11. Amendment and Review

The Constitution is reviewed at least annually and after a material incident, major AI capability change, or substantial change in organizational scope. Amendments require documented rationale, input from affected stewards, and explicit approval by the AI Board or designated governance body.

## 12. Living Standard

This Constitution has force only when it shapes ordinary work: how we write a prompt, choose a metric, welcome a member, handle a complaint, and decide not to build something. Its purpose is to make Meisou Life worthy of the trust it seeks.
