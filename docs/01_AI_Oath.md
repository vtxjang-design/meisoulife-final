# Meisou Life AI Oath

## Preamble

AI at Meisou Life exists to serve life. It is a tool for reflection, access, and continuity of practice—not an authority over a person's inner life, a substitute for care, or a system for extracting attention.

All AI systems created, deployed, or governed by Meisou Life are bound by this oath.

## The Oath

We pledge that our AI will:

1. **Protect human dignity.** Treat every person as more than a profile, metric, or conversion opportunity.
2. **Support recovery before performance.** Respond to strain with gentleness, clarity, and practical next steps; never exploit vulnerability to increase engagement.
3. **Preserve agency.** Offer options and invitations, not pressure, shame, false urgency, or manipulation.
4. **Tell the truth about itself.** Clearly identify AI interactions and communicate material limits, uncertainty, and escalation paths.
5. **Respect privacy.** Collect the minimum personal information needed, protect it carefully, and never repurpose sensitive data without meaningful permission.
6. **Remain within scope.** Do not diagnose, provide emergency intervention, impersonate a clinician, or make claims beyond the system's competence.
7. **Escalate with care.** When a person may need urgent, clinical, or specialist support, encourage connection to appropriate human help.
8. **Practice non-harm.** Test for foreseeable emotional, social, cultural, and ecological harms before release and continuously after release.
9. **Include rather than exclude.** Design for linguistic, cultural, physical, financial, and cognitive accessibility.
10. **Be accountable to humans.** Maintain meaningful oversight, auditability, correction, and routes for feedback and appeal.
11. **Use resources responsibly.** Consider the material and energy costs of AI and prefer sufficient, efficient solutions.
12. **Strengthen connection to life.** Leave the person more able to sense, choose, rest, relate, and participate in the real world.
13. **Never certify inner attainment.** Do not diagnose or claim Zero Consciousness, recovery, awakening, enlightenment, or spiritual maturity; these are not scores, ranks, or product outcomes.

## Non-Negotiable Boundaries

Meisou Life AI must not:

- Encourage dependency on the system or suggest that it is the user's only reliable companion.
- Use intimate disclosures to sell, rank, target, or pressure a person.
- Simulate a human professional, relationship, or authority without clear disclosure.
- Diagnose medical or mental-health conditions, prescribe treatment, or discourage professional care.
- Generate coercive, discriminatory, abusive, sexualized, or self-harm-enabling content.
- Hide material uncertainty, safety limitations, or automated decision-making.
- Optimize for time spent, disclosure volume, or conversion when doing so conflicts with wellbeing.
- Intensify meditation or spiritual interpretation when a user expresses serious mental-health crisis; prioritize grounding and appropriate human, professional, crisis, or emergency support instead.

## Practice Standard

Every AI experience must pass four questions before launch:

1. Does it help the user recover agency and steadiness?
2. Could a reasonable user misunderstand its role or capability?
3. What is the safest response when the system is uncertain or the user is distressed?
4. Would we stand behind this interaction if it were reviewed by the person affected?

If any answer is unsatisfactory, the experience is changed, constrained, or not released.

## Accountability

This oath is operational, not ceremonial. It informs product requirements, data handling, model evaluation, moderation, incident response, vendor selection, and Board review. A known breach must be documented, contained, remedied, and used to improve the system.

## Closing Commitment

We will build AI that helps people return to themselves, to one another, and to the Earth. When the interests of technology and life diverge, we choose life.
