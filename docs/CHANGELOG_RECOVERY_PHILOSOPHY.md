## Definitions Changed

- **Recovery:** Expanded from recovery of daily capacity to a process of restoring original nature, rhythm, conscious self-direction, relationship, and responsibility for life.
- **Zero Consciousness:** Defined as an open, centered state; explicitly not unconsciousness, numbness, passivity, dissociation, or escape.
- **Return to Origin:** Defined consistently as `復本`, restoration of original foundation.
- **Yullyeo:** Defined consistently as a philosophical concept of primordial rhythm and order, not a medical or scientific diagnosis.
- **Earth Citizenship:** Defined as an inclusive planetary identity.
- **Earth Management:** Defined consistently as life-serving stewardship, not ownership, control, domination, or administration of Earth.

## Terminology Decision

`復本` is the official Hanja form for 복본 in version 1.0. The glossary centralizes it as `RETURN_TO_ORIGIN_HANJA`. `複本` is not used; a founder-approved philosophical redefinition would require a controlled global revision.

## Safety Principles Added

- No scoring, ranking, certification, or leaderboard for recovery, consciousness, meditation, awakening, enlightenment, or spiritual maturity.
- No guarantee of transformation, healing, awakening, or enlightenment.
- No spiritual diagnosis by AI; no presentation of philosophy as medical or psychological treatment.
- No UX that promotes dependency, excessive use, guilt for absence, or withdrawal from real-world support.
- Serious distress requires grounding and appropriate professional, crisis, or emergency support—not meditation intensification or spiritual interpretation.

## Unresolved Questions Requiring Founder Approval

1. Should `Brain Ownership (뇌 주권)` remain an official co-term for `Consciousness Sovereignty (의식 주권)`, or should one be designated primary across all future materials?
2. Is the proposed Japanese rendering of `Return to Origin`—`本来への回帰`—the preferred philosophical translation, or should a project-specific transliteration be used?
3. Is `覺醒의 大衆化` the intended Hanja presentation for “Popularization of Enlightenment,” or should the term remain Korean-only without a formal Hanja expression?
4. Do founders intend a narrower or more specific traditional/philosophical definition of Yullyeo (律呂) than the neutral product definition used here?
5. Confirm whether `複本` will ever be adopted as a founder-defined term; it has not been assumed.
6. The high-level request omits `Awareness` from the internal growth map, while the detailed implementation directive includes it. Version 1.0 retains `Awareness` as an explicit non-ranking stage because it is required in HROS and the detailed directive; confirm the canonical internal-map wording.

## Translation Issues

- “Zero Consciousness” and “Yullyeo” are project-specific philosophical terms; translations must retain the non-clinical, non-quantified meaning.
- “Earth Management” can imply control in English and Japanese; every translated occurrence must retain the stewardship clarification.
- “Enlightenment” can imply certification or religious doctrine; user-facing copy should normally prefer “awareness,” “reflection,” or “conscious living.”

## Inconsistencies Discovered and Resolved

- Earlier HROS language focused on functional recovery only; it is now aligned with the wider recovery philosophy.
- Earlier Earth Management language did not explicitly rule out domination or ownership; this is now explicit.
- “Brain Ownership” and “Consciousness Sovereignty” appeared as competing terms in the implementation directive; they are retained as paired terms pending founder selection.
