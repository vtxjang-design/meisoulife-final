# Symbiosis Network Architecture

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Foundational V2 governance standard
**Purpose:** Define the voluntary social architecture through which personal recovery may become living fields of symbiosis.

## 1. Constitutional Purpose

Meisou Life supports people in recovering their Original Rhythm and freely carrying that recovery into relationships, communities, and care for the Earth. The Symbiosis Network is not a recruitment organization, spiritual hierarchy, or system of organizational control. It is a set of living fields in which people may connect through Gongshim and practice coexistence.

The canonical civilization pathway is:

```text
Recovery → Symbiosis → Earth Management → Spiritual Civilization
```

The Spiritual Civilization Meisou Life seeks to create is expressed in lived reality as Coexistence Civilization. Earth Citizenship is an identity, consciousness, and practice that supports the transition from Symbiosis toward Earth Management; it is not a competing civilization stage.

## 2. Canonical Terms

### 2.1 Gongshim

**Working Korean/Hanja form for governance review:** `공심 (公心)`
**Working English expression:** *shared-concern consciousness* or *heart of shared concern*.

Gongshim is the consciousness and heart of connection and shared concern: an orientation in which a person recognizes their wellbeing as related to the dignity, freedom, and wellbeing of others, the community, and the Earth. It supports listening, reciprocity, responsibility, and voluntary contribution without erasing personal boundaries or difference.

The proposed Hanja `公心` and English rendering require Korean-language and governance validation before they become glossary constants or required public-facing terms. Until then, use the semantic definition above and do not imply that Gongshim is synonymous with Gongsaeng.

### 2.2 Gongsaeng / Coexistence

**Gongsaeng (공생 / 共生) / Coexistence** is the way Gongshim becomes embodied in relationships and life: living and flourishing together in reciprocal responsibility. Gongshim is the consciousness or heart of connection; Gongsaeng is its lived relational and social practice. Neither term implies forced harmony, ideological conformity, or tolerance of harm.

### 2.3 Recovery Model

A **Recovery Model** is a person whose freely chosen daily practice has become visible as grounded, life-serving change in the way they live and relate. This is not a title, rank, certification, recruitment role, or proof of spiritual attainment. No person is required to become a Recovery Model, and it confers no authority, economic privilege, or responsibility to recruit.

### 2.4 Symbiosis Social OS

The **Symbiosis Social OS** is the consent-based social architecture of connected Local Symbiosis Cells, Units, and global relationships. It supports reciprocal practice, shared care, repair, and Earth-conscious participation. It does not manage people, assign spiritual levels, or require a person to progress through organizational layers.

### 2.5 Network Units

- **LSC — Local Symbiosis Cell:** an approximately 10-person voluntary living field of shared recovery and coexistence practice.
- **LSU — Local Symbiosis Unit:** an approximately 100-person network formed through connected LSCs.
- **GSU — Global Symbiosis Unit:** an approximately 1,000-person network formed through connected LSUs.

These sizes are orientation points, not quotas or thresholds of achievement. Expansion is not the goal; it is the natural result of healthy symbiosis.

## 3. Relationship to HROS

HROS is the individual Human Recovery Operating System. The Symbiosis Social OS extends, but never replaces, personal recovery.

```text
HROS → Personal Recovery → Recovery Model → LSC → LSU → GSU
```

This diagram describes possible, freely chosen relationships—not a mandatory ladder. A person may practice individually, participate in an LSC without becoming a Recovery Model, pause participation, or leave without penalty.

## 4. Core Field Principles

- Do not teach from authority — reflect.
- Do not lead through control — choose together.
- Do not control — maintain awareness.
- Protect consent, dignity, privacy, and the right to pause or leave.
- Return attention to daily life, relationships, community, nature, and responsible action.

Never implement leader worship, follower structures, spiritual hierarchy, recruitment competition, downline incentives, growth-based authority, coercive engagement, economic dependency, or pressure to recruit members. Bringing more people must never create power or status.

## 5. Facilitation and Field Keeping

Use **LSC Facilitator** or **Field Keeper**, not “Leader,” unless an older context is explicitly being discussed and its meaning is clarified.

An LSC Facilitator or Field Keeper exists to protect the health of the living field: predictable practice, consent, inclusion, boundaries, listening, and repair. The role does not command people and carries no spiritual status, recruitment privilege, growth-based authority, or automatic organizational power. Responsibilities must be transparent, reviewable, time-bounded, and transferable.

## 6. Participation and Safety

Participation is voluntary and must be understandable before a person joins. Members may decline sharing, change groups, pause, or leave without guilt, loss of dignity, or loss-framed pressure. Personal disclosures are not participation requirements.

Groups must provide clear expectations for confidentiality, respectful disagreement, reporting harm, boundaries, and referral to appropriate human, professional, or emergency support when needs exceed the group’s scope. No LSC, LSU, GSU, facilitator, or AI may provide clinical diagnosis, spiritual diagnosis, crisis intervention, or coercive direction.

## 7. Share Recovery

Share Recovery is an invitation to offer another person a free recovery experience, not a referral or acquisition mechanism. Its preferred path is:

```text
1-Minute Recovery → 7-Day Recovery → voluntary membership → LSC participation
```

Do not attach rewards, commissions, quotas, status, conversion targets, downlines, or social pressure to sharing. A receiver’s recovery and informed choice come before any membership or community invitation.

## 8. Measures of Field Health

Assess fields through voluntary participation, felt safety, clarity of consent, reciprocal contribution, respectful repair, ability to pause or leave, and evidence that people return to daily life with more agency and care. Include ecological and community effects where material.

Never use membership growth, attendance, recruitment volume, disclosure volume, spiritual status, or time on the platform as a proxy for field health.

## 9. Governance and Review

Material network features, incentives, data practices, or automated decisions require the Mandatory Decision Test, risk assessment, and the safeguards defined in the Operating Constitution. This architecture is reviewed after Founding LSU #001 validation and whenever evidence suggests pressure, dependency, hierarchy, privacy harm, or mission drift.
