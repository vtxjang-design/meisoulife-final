# Local Symbiosis Cell Charter

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Foundational V2 community charter
**Purpose:** Establish the minimum safeguards for an LSC as a voluntary living field of shared recovery and coexistence.

## 1. Purpose

An LSC (Local Symbiosis Cell) is an approximately 10-person, voluntary field where people may practice recovery, connect through Gongshim, and embody coexistence in ordinary life. It exists to support agency, reciprocal care, and responsible participation in life—not to create a following, spiritual rank, recruitment channel, or economic dependency.

An LSC is one possible social expression of HROS. Personal practice remains valid without LSC participation.

## 2. Participation Rights

Every participant may:

- join, decline, pause, change groups, or leave without penalty or shame;
- choose how much, if anything, to share;
- receive clear information about the group’s purpose, practices, expectations, and data handling;
- disagree respectfully and maintain personal boundaries;
- report harm and receive a meaningful response; and
- seek support outside the group, including professional or emergency support when needed.

No one must disclose personal, spiritual, medical, financial, or relationship information to belong.

## 3. Field Practices

An LSC may offer simple recovery practices, reflection, listening, gratitude, shared learning, and voluntary Earth-conscious action. Practices must be accessible, permission-based, culturally humble, and connected to daily life.

Use invitation language. Do not claim that a participant has reached Zero Consciousness, awakening, enlightenment, recovery, or a higher stage. Do not present contemplative practice as medical or psychological treatment.

## 4. Facilitation

The preferred role name is **LSC Facilitator** or **Field Keeper**. Its purpose is to protect the health of the living field by holding predictable structure, consent, inclusion, boundaries, listening, and repair.

The role has no spiritual status, recruitment privilege, growth-based authority, automatic organizational power, or right to command personal decisions. Facilitation responsibilities must be transparent, reviewable, time-bounded, and transferable.

## 5. Prohibited Structures and Conduct

An LSC must not create or tolerate:

- leader worship or follower structures;
- spiritual hierarchy, certification, scoring, or attainment claims;
- pressure to attend, disclose, agree, donate, purchase, recruit, or remain;
- recruitment competition, quotas, downlines, commissions, or conversion targets;
- economic dependency or undisclosed financial benefit;
- coercion, humiliation, discrimination, manipulation, or retaliation; or
- AI, facilitator, or peer behavior that replaces professional care or controls a person’s consciousness.

## 6. Privacy, Safety, and Repair

Set clear confidentiality expectations at entry. Collect only the information necessary to support voluntary participation; do not use personal disclosures as marketing, targeting, or recruitment material. Explain exceptions to confidentiality clearly, including imminent safety concerns and legal obligations where applicable.

When harm occurs, receive reports without retaliation, assess fairly, protect affected people, and use proportionate repair. Responses may include clarification, apology, changed practice, facilitator support, access restriction, referral, or dissolution of the field. Serious safety, medical, mental-health, abuse, or crisis concerns require appropriate human, professional, crisis, or emergency support—not intensified spiritual practice.

## 7. Relationship to LSU, GSU, and Share Recovery

Connected LSCs may form an LSU, and connected LSUs may form a GSU. These connections do not create a chain of command. No unit gains authority over an individual’s beliefs, private life, money, or membership choices.

Share Recovery means offering a free recovery experience. It is not recruitment. Any invitation must begin with 1-Minute Recovery, then 7-Day Recovery, followed only by voluntary membership and LSC participation. No incentives or pressure may be attached.

## 8. Field Health and Validation

Evaluate an LSC by whether people can participate voluntarily; feel respected and safe; maintain boundaries; practice reciprocal care; repair harm; return to ordinary life with agency; and participate in Earth-conscious practice where freely chosen.

Do not use attendance, accounts, recruitment, time spent, spiritual experience, or growth as the primary measure of health. A field that creates pressure, dependency, hierarchy, or loss of agency must pause, repair, or dissolve rather than scale.

## 9. Review and Amendment

This charter is reviewed during Founding LSU #001 validation and after material incidents. Amendments require documented rationale, input from affected participants and facilitators, and review under the Operating Constitution. The charter does not replace applicable law, safeguarding obligations, or professional-care requirements.
