# Meisou Life V2 Vision

**Version:** 2.0
**Status:** Foundational V2 vision and prioritization standard
**Purpose:** Express the V2 direction without replacing the Constitution, HROS, Earth Management OS, or canonical glossary.

## 1. V2 Purpose

Meisou Life helps people recover their Original Rhythm, make recovery visible in everyday life and relationships, connect through Gongshim, form living fields of symbiosis, and participate in the creation of a Coexistence Civilization.

Recovery begins freely. Membership supports continuity.

## 2. Canonical Civilization Architecture

```text
Recovery → Symbiosis → Earth Management → Spiritual Civilization
```

The Spiritual Civilization Meisou Life seeks to create is expressed in lived reality as Coexistence Civilization.

Spiritual Civilization names the consciousness, values, and civilizational orientation: dignity of life, agency, shared responsibility, and care for the Earth. Coexistence Civilization names how those values are embodied in human relationships, communities, technology, economy, culture, society, and relationship with the Earth.

Earth Citizenship is an inclusive identity, consciousness, and practice that supports the transition from Symbiosis toward Earth Management. It is not a separate competing civilization stage.

## 3. Original Rhythm Vocabulary

**Original Rhythm** is the canonical V2 term: the fundamental rhythm through which a person’s Original Nature is expressed in life.

- **Original Nature** is the philosophical description of a human being’s inherent dignity, vitality, clarity, compassion, responsibility, and capacity for conscious choice.
- **Original Rhythm** is the lived expression of that Original Nature through breath, rest, movement, emotion, relationships, attention, nature, and daily life.
- **Rhythm Alignment** is the process of returning toward alignment with Original Rhythm.
- **Natural Rhythm** is accessible user-facing language where simpler wording is appropriate.

These terms are related but not arbitrary synonyms. Their addition to the canonical glossary requires a controlled follow-up update.

## 4. Human and Awakening Standard

An awakened person is someone who observes themselves, recovers their Original Rhythm, chooses as the owner of their own brain and life, and practices coexistence with people and the Earth.

Awakening is not a rank, certification, or spiritual status. It is a way of living and choosing. Meisou Life does not assess, infer, certify, score, or promise awakening, Zero Consciousness, recovery, or spiritual maturity.

Popularization of Enlightenment means bringing opportunities for awareness, reflection, and conscious choice into ordinary life. It must never create spiritual ranking, enlightenment certification, spiritual superiority, dependency on leaders or AI, or coercive belief structures.

## 5. V2 Product Principle and Journey

**EXPERIENCE FIRST. EXPLANATION SECOND.**

The primary V2 journey is:

```text
Homepage
→ 1-Minute Recovery
→ 7-Day Recovery
→ Free Daily Rhythm Introduction
→ BASIC Sustained Daily Rhythm
→ LSC
→ Recovery Model
→ Share Recovery
→ New Person's 1-Minute Recovery
```

The Free Daily Rhythm Introduction helps a person discover that recovery can become a daily rhythm. BASIC Sustained Daily Rhythm supports deeper continuity, practice, personalization, and sustained rhythm. Payment must not be placed in front of the initial recovery experience.

Every transition remains voluntary, understandable, accessible, and easy to pause or leave. Advanced concepts—including Zero Consciousness, Return to Origin (復本), and Yullyeo—remain optional, progressively introduced, and clearly distinct from clinical or scientific claims.

## 6. Growth and Sharing Standard

Expansion is not the goal. Expansion is the natural result of healthy symbiosis.

Success is not registered accounts alone. It increasingly means that recovery, voluntary participation, healthy living fields, natural Recovery Sharing, human agency, and Earth-conscious practice are actually alive.

Members are never turned into recruiters. Share Recovery offers a free recovery experience before any voluntary membership or LSC participation. It must never use quotas, downlines, commissions, status, conversion pressure, or economic dependency.
