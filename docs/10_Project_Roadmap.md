# Meisou Life Project Roadmap

**Version:** 1.0
**Planning horizon:** Foundation to durable, regenerative operations

## 1. Roadmap Principle

The roadmap is ordered by trust: first make the core experience safe and reliable; then deepen recovery and belonging; then expand carefully. No phase is complete solely because features ship. Completion requires evidence that the experience is stable, understandable, and aligned with the Foundation Pack.

## 2. Phase 1 — Foundation and Reliability

**Objective:** Establish a dependable, calm, multilingual core.

- Stabilize authentication, membership recognition, payment flows, and account support.
- Ensure Japanese, Korean, and English interfaces preserve meaning and safety language.
- Deliver a clear free-entry practice and reliable member access.
- Establish content quality standards based on HROS.
- Implement baseline privacy, security, analytics, incident, and release practices.
- Adopt the Foundation Pack as the governing reference.

**Exit signals:** Critical user paths are verified; support and billing states are understandable; high-risk data flows are mapped; the team can release and roll back safely.

## 3. Phase 2 — Daily Recovery Experience

**Objective:** Make small, repeatable practice genuinely useful in daily life.

- Expand guided practice journeys with calm pacing and accessible controls.
- Introduce optional, user-controlled rhythms for reminders and return.
- Build reflective progress experiences that avoid shame and compulsion.
- Improve narration, audio, and content consistency through native-language review.
- Gather qualitative feedback on steadiness, usability, and trust.

**Exit signals:** Users can begin, pause, return, and find a suitable next practice without confusion or pressure.

## 4. Phase 3 — Gongsaeng AI and Community Care

**Objective:** Introduce bounded AI support and strengthen belonging responsibly.

- Establish the AI Executive Council, Human AI Governance Review, risk tiers, evaluation suite, and incident process.
- Release AI guidance only within the AI Oath, HROS, and Gongsaeng AI Guide.
- Implement clear AI disclosure, privacy controls, and human escalation pathways.
- Strengthen community standards, moderator support, reporting, and repair practices.
- Develop pathways from solo practice to voluntary shared participation.

**Exit signals:** AI behavior is tested across normal, ambiguous, and distress scenarios; community safety processes are active; users understand where AI ends and human support begins.

## 5. Phase 4 — Regenerative Ecosystem

**Objective:** Extend Meisou Life from a platform into a durable culture of coexistence.

- Develop retreat, facilitator, and partner programs aligned with Earth Management OS.
- Create contribution pathways for members without status pressure or extraction.
- Assess material infrastructure, vendor, event, and AI impacts.
- Publish appropriate learning and accountability summaries.
- Build partnerships that strengthen local communities and ecological care.

**Exit signals:** Growth demonstrates benefits beyond digital engagement and maintains clear environmental and community stewardship.

## 6. Ongoing Workstreams

| Workstream | Continuous responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Trust infrastructure | Auth, payment, membership, support, privacy, security |
| Practice quality | HROS content, accessibility, multilingual integrity, feedback |
| AI stewardship | Oath compliance, evaluation, oversight, incident learning |
| Community | Norms, facilitation, moderation, repair, belonging |
| Earth stewardship | Sufficient infrastructure, responsible vendors, regenerative choices |
| Organizational learning | Documentation, review, governance, capability building |

## 7. Prioritization Framework

Prioritize work by asking:

1. Does it materially improve human recovery, agency, or trust?
2. Does it reduce a meaningful safety, reliability, privacy, or ecological risk?
3. Is the scope small enough to validate responsibly?
4. Are dependencies, owners, and operating costs understood?
5. Does it align with the Operating Constitution?

Items that merely increase novelty, pressure, or opaque engagement should not displace foundational work.

## 8. Review Cadence

- Review operational reliability and user safety continuously.
- Review roadmap priorities quarterly.
- Review Foundation Pack alignment annually and after material incidents or capability changes.
- Treat feedback from members, stewards, and affected communities as roadmap evidence.

## 9. Definition of Progress

Meisou Life progresses when more people can return to themselves without losing agency; when community becomes more trustworthy; when technology becomes more accountable; and when the organization becomes more capable of serving life over the long term.
