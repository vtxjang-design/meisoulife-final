# Gongsaeng Platform Governance

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Foundational governance standard

**Purpose:** Define the philosophical source, human authority, Gongsaeng AI operating system, AI Executive Council, and governance boundaries of Meisou Life.

## 1. Canonical Platform Definition

Meisou Life is a coexistence platform where people recover their Original Rhythm, allow recovery to become visible in everyday life and relationships, connect through Gongshim, form living fields of symbiosis, practice Earth Management, and participate in creating Spiritual Civilization as lived Coexistence Civilization.

Meisou Life is not only a meditation service or wellness platform. It integrates meditation, daily rhythm, AI, membership, community, sustainable economics, and Earth Management into a practical life system.

## 2. Philosophical Source

The canonical Korean expression is:

> **일지 이승헌 스승님의 철학적 원류(지구경영과 뇌교육)**

Meisou Life draws from Ilchi Lee's Earth Management and Brain Education, including Hongik, Brain Ownership, natural intelligence, Earth Citizenship, Earth Management, coexistence culture, and the direction of Spiritual Civilization in the AI era.

Philosophical attribution does not automatically establish endorsement, official participation, platform-founding status, or permission to use names, images, trademarks, or copyrighted works. External communication must remain within documented permission.

## 3. Civilization Architect

Master Jang is the **Civilization Architect of Meisou Life**.

The role translates the philosophical source into:

- HROS and the recovery journey;

## 8. Human AI Governance Review

Human review remains mandatory for risk-bearing decisions. Review should include, proportionate to impact:

- the accountable executive sponsor;
- product and engineering responsibility;
- safety, privacy, legal, or governance expertise;
- HROS practitioner or community-care knowledge;
- affected-user or culturally relevant perspective; and
- independent expertise when impact or scale warrants it.

Human reviewers may require safeguards, a smaller pilot, evaluation, monitoring, rollback, or a safety pause within delegated responsibilities. Qualified professional review is required for material legal, clinical, privacy, accounting, tax, trademark, copyright, and other regulated matters.

## 9. Growth Constitution

> Expansion is not the goal; it is the natural result of healthy symbiosis.

> Failure is not failing to increase membership. Failure is losing awareness and the health of the living field.

Growth systems must not create recruitment quotas, downlines, commissions, status, conversion pressure, spiritual hierarchy, leader worship, economic dependency, or authority based on bringing more people.

The official 2035 cultural-reach vision remains 10 million people. Designing for 100 million means technical and governance scalability beyond that horizon; it is not a current acquisition target and does not override the 2035 vision.

## 10. Mandatory Decision Test

Before implementation, record:

1. Does this align with the philosophical source and the Constitution?
2. Does it help a person recover their Original Rhythm?
3. Does it strengthen natural intelligence, Brain Ownership, and agency?
4. Does personal recovery become healthier daily life, relationship, or community practice?
5. Does it support healthy symbiosis without dependence, hierarchy, coercion, or recruitment pressure?
6. Can it lead toward Earth Citizenship and Earth Management through practical choice?
7. Does AI support rather than replace, evaluate, or control the human being?
8. Is experience offered before advanced explanation?
9. Are data collection, retention, access, and vendor roles minimal and understandable?
10. Is the system sustainable and scalable without sacrificing dignity, awareness, safety, and trust?
11. What are the affected people, foreseeable risks, safeguards, rollback, stop conditions, and non-engagement success measures?

If a conflict remains unresolved, implementation is **HOLD**.
