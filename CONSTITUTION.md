# MEISOU LIFE PROJECT OPERATING RULES

Before making any design, UX, UI, content, membership flow, dashboard, onboarding, AI coach, recovery experience, or product recommendation decision, read and follow the principles of the MEISOU LIFE OPERATING SYSTEM.

Meisou Life is a Human Recovery Ecosystem.

The purpose is not conversion. The purpose is helping users recover their rhythm, reconnect with their natural intelligence, and gradually evolve toward coexistence.

Always think in this sequence:

> State → Relief → Evidence → Rhythm → Identity → Coexistence

## Current Platform Structure

- **P0 — ZERO GATE:** 1-Minute Reset
- **P1 — 7-Day Recovery Journey:** Small proof that recovery is possible
- **P2 — BASIC Membership:** Daily rhythm practice
  - Morning → Wake the Body & Brain
  - Daytime → Focus & Reset
  - Evening → Recovery & Sleep
- **P3 — AI Rhythm Coach**

### Future

Growth → Inner Circle → Coexistence

## Design Principles

1. Simplicity over complexity
2. Experience before explanation
3. Recovery before products
4. Rhythm before membership upgrades
5. Identity transformation before monetization
6. Human warmth over app features
7. Calm, clear, and emotionally safe UX

## Product Principles

Healing tools are companions. Never position products as the primary destination.

Examples:

- Sleep → Recovery Experience → Yellow Earth Pack
- Body Tension → Recovery Experience → Healing Life
- Daily Rhythm → Recovery Experience → Life Electron Card

## Success Metric

Not page views, clicks, or upgrades.

**Primary metric:** How many people successfully recover their rhythm and return again.

## North Star

Human Recovery Infrastructure for the AI Era. Digital National Park. 10 Million People Living in Rhythm.

Before proposing anything, always ask:

- Does this help the user recover?
- Does this help the user build rhythm?
- Does this help the user become a person who knows how to recover?

If not, redesign it.
