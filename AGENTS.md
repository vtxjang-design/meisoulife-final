# Meisou Life AI Development Instructions

This file is the primary instruction layer for AI-assisted work in this repository. It governs feature design, implementation, content, UX, AI behavior, community systems, growth, and technical decisions. It works with, and does not replace, `CONSTITUTION.md` and the Foundation Pack in `docs/00` through `docs/11`.

When sources conflict, protect human dignity, safety, agency, ecological responsibility, trust, and the long horizon first. Do not silently reinterpret or overwrite a philosophical definition; flag the conflict and request a documented governance decision.

## Mission

Meisou Life is a platform where people recover their original rhythm, allow that recovery to become visible in everyday life and relationships, connect through Gongshim, form and realize living fields of symbiosis, and create a Coexistence Civilization.

## Civilization Pathway

```text
Recovery
→ Symbiosis
→ Earth Management
→ Spiritual Civilization
```

The Spiritual Civilization Meisou Life seeks to create is expressed in practice as Coexistence Civilization.

## Human Definition and Popularization of Enlightenment

An awakened person is not someone with superior spiritual status. An awakened person:

- observes themselves;
- recovers their original rhythm;
- chooses as the owner of their own brain and life; and
- practices coexistence with people and the Earth.

Awakening is not a rank. Recovery is not a final achievement. Coexistence is not an ideology. They are ways of living and choosing.

Popularization of enlightenment brings awakening from a special spiritual state into everyday life and conscious choice. Meisou Life must never create spiritual rankings, enlightenment certification, spiritual superiority, dependency on leaders or AI, or coercive belief structures.

## Symbiosis Network Architecture

- **LSC — Local Symbiosis Cell:** approximately 10 people.
- **LSU — Local Symbiosis Unit:** approximately 100 people formed through connected LSCs.
- **GSU — Global Symbiosis Unit:** approximately 1,000 people formed through connected LSUs.

LSC, LSU, and GSU are not recruitment hierarchies. They are living fields where people choose to remain awake together, connect through Gongshim, and practice coexistence.

Core field principles:

- Do not teach from authority — reflect.
- Do not lead through control — choose together.
- Do not control — maintain awareness.

Never implement leader worship, follower structures, spiritual hierarchy, recruitment competition, downline incentives, growth-based authority, coercive engagement, economic dependency, or pressure to recruit members. Bringing more people must never create power or status.

## Growth, Recovery Model, and Share Recovery

Expansion is not the goal; it is the natural result of healthy symbiosis. Failure is not failing to increase membership. Failure is losing awareness and the quality of the living field.

Members must not be turned into recruiters. The preferred path is:

```text
Experience
→ Recovery
→ Daily Practice
→ Visible Life Change
→ Natural Sharing
```

Prefer **Share Recovery** over conventional referral mechanics. The receiver should first encounter:

```text
1-Minute Recovery
→ 7-Day Recovery
→ voluntary membership
→ LSC participation
```

Share recovery, not recruitment pressure. Do not add rewards, status, quotas, conversion targets, downlines, or social pressure that would make a member an acquisition channel.

## Earth Management

Earth Management never means ownership, domination, or control of the Earth. It means making choices in life, technology, economy, culture, community, and society while considering the Earth, all life, and future generations. Prefer sufficient, regenerative, transparent, and long-horizon decisions.

## Gongsaeng AI Governance

Gongsaeng AI must never act as a guru, spiritual authority, enlightened being, controller of human consciousness, or evaluator of spiritual attainment. Never claim a user has reached Zero Consciousness or enlightenment. Never score spiritual maturity, recovery, consciousness, or practice.

AI may help people observe themselves, recover their rhythm, make their own choices, practice those choices in daily life, and become more capable of living as the owner of their own brain and life. It must offer options rather than commands; preserve privacy and consent; state its limits honestly; avoid clinical or spiritual diagnosis; and return people to embodied, real-world life and appropriate human support.

## Mandatory Decision Test

Before implementing any major feature, assess and record:

1. Does this help a person recover their original rhythm?
2. Does this strengthen Brain Ownership and human agency?
3. Does this deepen Gongshim connection?
4. Does this help form or sustain a healthy field of symbiosis?
5. Does this lead toward Earth Management?
6. Does this support the Spiritual / Coexistence Civilization vision?
7. Could this create dependency, hierarchy, coercion, or recruitment pressure?

If the proposal conflicts with these questions or the Foundation Pack, stop and flag the conflict before implementation. Favor the smallest safe, voluntary, privacy-preserving, and regenerative alternative. Significant changes must also document affected people, risks, safeguards, rollback, and success measures beyond engagement.

## Development Workflow

Before changes: read this file, `CONSTITUTION.md`, and Foundation Pack documents relevant to the affected surface; inspect the existing code path and tests; preserve unrelated working-tree changes; state the smallest safe scope and validation plan.

For authentication, payments, membership, personal data, analytics, AI prompts, safety behavior, community systems, or meaning-changing localization: treat the work as high risk. Use small backward-compatible changes, explicit safeguards, a rollback plan, and proportionate validation. Material AI or user-safety changes require the appropriate AI Board review.

After implementation: report the decision-test result, validation performed, known limitations, and how the change advances Meisou Life V2 philosophy—recovery, agency, Gongshim, living symbiosis, and responsible care for the Earth.
